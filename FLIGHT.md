# Subterranea 0.9 - flight and impact guide

18 November 2026

## Cockpit and chase camera

Press T during flight to switch between cockpit and chase views. The action is remappable in Flight Settings > Controls. Touchpad click is the default gamepad event; controllers without that button can rebind it or use Pause > Flight Settings > View. The HUD shows the current view. Camera choice is stored in shared flight.cfg settings, independently of campaign profiles and manual mission snapshots.

Chase view follows the actual painted Kestrel through full six-axis rotation. A 0.25-meter sphere cast checks the camera path against world and door collision. The camera retracts immediately near obstructions, eases outward when clear, and temporarily uses cockpit view if the remaining distance is under 1.05 meters. The chase preference stays enabled so the exterior returns automatically. Boost gives the camera more distance and retains the existing FOV effect. Camera shake stays configurable.

The exterior model is scaled to fit within the existing flight collision volume. Ship handling, movement collision and damage statistics retain their definitions. Pulse, plasma, rail and missiles originate at the ship, converge toward the chase reticle and check intervening cover. Seeing over a wall with the camera does not let a weapon shoot through it. First-person weapon handling remains available at any time.

## Engines and damage

Engine plumes ease from idle to thrust and strengthen during boost. Damaged engines flicker below half integrity. The new engine/afterburner sound layers respond to movement and boost; see SOUND.md for the sound bank.

Incoming hits trigger a directional shield ripple and impact sparks. Below 55% integrity, localized scorch marks appear; below 45%, ships emit smoke, becoming more frequent below 25%. These visuals use existing shield/health/integrity values and add no separate hull-health system. Repairing clears the damaged appearance and engine flicker. Checkpoint restoration derives the appearance from restored values. The rescue courier remains repairable when disabled.

## Breakup effects

Player, drone, Warden and Overseer deaths produce armor and engine chunks from their actual authored hull geometry. Each model has six reusable fragments, generated by tools/author_debris.py. Engine pieces leave short smoke trails. Cosmetic pieces have no physics collision bodies, do not block flight and cannot damage or reward another actor. Ray casts allow them to bounce off walls. They shrink out after roughly 2.6-4 seconds.

Active hull-piece budgets are 24 / 48 / 72 for Performance / Balanced / Quality. Damage-smoke emission stops when the shared visual-effect pool reaches 180 entries. Existing blast sparks and shock rings are separate effects. Map and pause freeze effect movement and damage-state timers; purely shader-driven exhaust shimmer is cosmetic. Existing pieces can finish animating beneath victory/loss overlays. Pieces and temporary smoke are not saved in mission checkpoints.

## Validation

The presentation suite checks camera orientation and real sphere clearance, legacy T binding precedence, default pad events, boost response, directional damage, repair, pause, actual target hits from all four weapons, a raised-camera/low-cover case, exact hull chunks, duplicate death guards, the fragment budget, wall bounces, expiry, checkpoint appearance and final boss/player death flow.

All three operations also complete through real flight, objectives, boss phases and extraction with chase requested. Those geometry scenarios use high test shields and frozen/nonblocking ordinary enemies; they verify routes and objective flow rather than human combat survival. Normal input/controller tests are simulated except for real display mouse capture; no physical gamepad is claimed.

Eleven staged engine captures under exports/flight-preview cover chase, boost, shield hits, smoke, enemy damage, breakup, fragments, clearance, bindings and the updated sound/view settings. Short-lived effects are held at a representative animation point for some captures. Actual gameplay timers are checked separately.
